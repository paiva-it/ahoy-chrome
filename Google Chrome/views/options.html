Ahoy! Personal

A little more open

Choose the websites that need a different connection. Everything else keeps using your normal connection.

1. Your connection

Double-click Start Ahoy Helper.command in the download folder and leave its window open. The helper uses Cloudflare encrypted DNS. Your public IP stays the same.

Use a trusted proxy you control. Username/password authentication is not supported.

2. Your websites

Each domain includes all its subdomains. You can also add the current site from the toolbar popup. If a page needs a separate media or login domain, add that domain too.
